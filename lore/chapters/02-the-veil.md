# The Veil

**Status:** live

## The place
Beneath the moat lies the spirit realm: the same land, swaying like a dream, washed violet, with falling code in the air. Life slowly fades there.

## What the player does
Step through with Q, devour the souls of fallen foes to stay alive, face the godlings Aurelion the Sunless and Varghast the Hollow Thunderer in the outer wilds, and return through a Veil Rift.

## The shard
No shard yet. The Veil may hold one in a later chapter.
