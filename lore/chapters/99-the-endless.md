# The Endless

When every shard is gathered, the World Heart glows whole for a moment, and then the worlds begin to drift again.

This is by design. Moonlit Moat never ends:
- The world tier rises every two minutes, and bosses, creeps and treasure grow with it.
- Environments turn (Blood Moon, Creeping Mist, Aurora, Ember Storm), the maze reshuffles, the Warden hunts.
- New shards can be found at any time: every contributor who adds a world adds a chapter.

## Ascension
Felling the Gorgon and the Empress is not an ending. The moat ascends: the edge of its land breaks outward (80 m, then 102, 124, 146 and 168), uncovering old forest, broken stone circles and sealed chests. The wild grows fiercer, and after a short lull the queens rise again and walk the land, stronger, until the Wanderer calls them back to the throne.

## The mythics
As time passes, great creatures of myth wake far from the castle: the Minotaur, the Oni, the Manticore, the Basilisk, the Kitsune, the Cyclops and the Hydra. After the first Ascension, three tyrants of this world's own follow them:
- **Admiral Saltmarrow**, a drowned admiral who walks out of the lake dragging his anchor.
- **Vorrask, the Hollow Sovereign**, a masked king of the void, risen from what the Veil devoured.
- **Kael Thurnis, the Star-Devourer**, a horned conqueror from beyond the sky who burns worlds for sport.

When you add something, ask: what moment does this create for the player? That moment is the storyline.
