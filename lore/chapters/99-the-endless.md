# The Endless

When every shard is gathered, the World Heart glows whole for a moment, and then the worlds begin to drift again.

This is by design. Moonlit Moat never ends:
- The world tier rises every two minutes, and bosses, creeps and treasure grow with it.
- Environments turn (Blood Moon, Creeping Mist, Aurora, Ember Storm), the maze reshuffles, the Warden hunts.
- New shards can be found at any time: every contributor who adds a world adds a chapter.

When you add something, ask: what moment does this create for the player? That moment is the storyline.
