# The Moonlit Moat

**Status:** live · **Season 1, The Broken Wedding:** in development

## The place
A keep on a lake under a huge moon, guarded by the Gate Warden. On its roof, Vesthra the Gorgon and Nyxara the Thorn Empress hold court; above it, the dragon Cinderwing circles and sets off fireworks.

## Season 1: The Broken Wedding
Every hundred years two queens of the moat are wed on the castle roof under a full moon. This time it was Nyxara and Vesthra. At the vows the moon cracked in two, and both brides turned on the one person sworn to guard them: the Wanderer.

- **The cracked moon** hangs over the moat from the start. Sometimes, when a queen rises again, something in its dark half opens and follows the Wanderer.
- **The brides hunt her.** Each time a queen falls, the real queen breaks through for a moment before she is taken again. Nyxara knows the Wanderer's name.
- **The Warden guards the gate** and hunts the Wanderer every few minutes. He will not say why.
- **Cinderwing** still sets off the wedding fireworks.
- **The statues** in the courtyard are wedding guests.
- **Ends on:** both brides fall, and the moon's dark half opens and looks down at the Wanderer. The Warden: "You should not have come back."

## What the player does
Grow strong on crawlers, wisps, ancients, chests and the shifting hedge maze; solve the five puzzles in the wildlands; ride a Moonstag, Gryphon or Ember Drake; challenge the Warden at the gate, then the brides on the roof.

## The shard
The Warden. Beating it grants Warden's Might and frees the moat's shard.
