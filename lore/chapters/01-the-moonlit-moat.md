# The Moonlit Moat

**Status:** live

## The place
A keep on a lake under a huge moon, guarded by the Gate Warden. On its roof, Vesthra the Gorgon and Nyxara the Thorn Empress hold court; above it, the dragon Cinderwing circles and sets off fireworks.

## What the player does
Grow strong on crawlers, wisps, ancients, chests and the shifting hedge maze; ride a Moonstag, Gryphon or Ember Drake; then challenge the Warden at the gate.

## The shard
The Warden. Beating it grants Warden's Might and frees the moat's shard.
