# Seasons

The story ships one season at a time, one world per season, each ending on a question the next one answers.
This page shows only what a released season has revealed; later seasons list a title and a teaser, nothing more.

## How a season ships
- **A chapter file** in `lore/chapters/` with the season's story, written when the season is released.
- **Story beats in the game:** banners, one-line memories found in the world, boss lines. No cutscenes.
- **One new puzzle or cross-world chain**, and the world's quest tied into the main story.
- **An ending beat** that points at the next world.
- **Release gate:** worlds that are already live stay open to everyone. A season unlocks the story beats for
  its world, so older players see the new story the next time they visit.
