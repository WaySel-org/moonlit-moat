# Canon of the Sundered Worlds

This is the shared truth every contributor writes against. The Loremaster keeps it.

## The premise
Before the game begins, something broke the **World Heart**, the bright core that held every world together. Reality split into **shards**. Each shard became a world of its own, cut off from the others and slowly forgetting it was ever whole. The Wanderer, a traveller with a lantern and a sword, keeps stepping between them.

## The rules of the story
- **There is no ending.** Gathering every shard mends the Heart for a moment, then the worlds drift again: tiers rise, environments turn, the maze reshuffles, the Warden hunts. The story is what happens on the way.
- **Every run carries a storyline.** What you fight, tame, find and survive is the story. Design for moments, not cutscenes.
- **Worlds are shards.** Each world has a chapter, an objective, a boss that holds its shard, and a Shardkeeper who maintains it.
- **Travel follows the story.** Finishing a chapter pulls you to the next shard (a story switch). World Gates let you return. Rarely, a rift storm drags you somewhere unplanned for a short while.
- **The spirit realm lies under every world.** It is where life fades and souls are devoured; so far only the moat's Veil has been opened.

## Tone
Quiet, moonlit, a little melancholy, never grim for its own sake. Enemies are strange rather than gory. Text is short and plain.

## Originality
Everything here is invented for this game. Myths and folklore are fair game (gorgons, gryphons, dragons); characters, names, designs and plots from existing games, films, shows and books are not.
